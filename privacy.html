Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Viral Clipper ("the App") is a content-scheduling tool that lets an authorized TikTok creator select video clips and publish them to their own TikTok account via TikTok's Content Posting API.

What data we access

When you authorize the App via TikTok Login Kit, we request:

  • user.info.basic — your TikTok username and avatar, shown in the App's interface so you can confirm which account content will be posted to.
  • video.publish — permission to publish video content to your own authorized TikTok account, only when you explicitly trigger a post.

We do not access, read, or store any other TikTok account data — not your followers, not your messages, and not other users' content.

What we store

  • Your TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens, stored locally on the operator's own machine and never transmitted to a third party.
  • Video files you choose to cut and publish, stored locally until posted.
  • Basic post metadata: title, an internal clip score, timestamps, and — once recorded — view, like, and comment counts for your own posts.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers, do not sell or share your data, and do not use your data to train any third-party model.

Data retention and deletion

All data is stored locally by the account operator. You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings, which invalidates its tokens. You may also request deletion of stored tokens and post history by contacting us at the address below.

Turn your long-form video into short clips — and publish them to your own TikTok account.

Viral Clipper is a scheduling tool for creators who publish short-form video. It cuts long recordings into vertical clips with captions, and publishes them directly to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  • Pick a source video. Point the tool at a long-form video you own or have the rights to use.
  • Get clip suggestions. The tool builds a transcript and scores candidate segments for short-form potential, then proposes the strongest ones.
  • Cut to vertical. Selected segments are cut to a 9:16 frame with readable burned-in captions.
  • Connect TikTok. You authorize the tool with your TikTok account. It only ever posts to accounts you have explicitly connected.
  • Publish or schedule. Every post is triggered by you, shows which account it will go to, and requires you to pick a privacy level before it can be sent.

What it does not do

  • It never posts without an explicit action from you for that specific post.
  • It does not read your messages, your followers, or anyone else's content.
  • It does not add its own branding, logo, or watermark to anything published to TikTok.
  • It does not sell, share, or monetize your data.

Rights and responsibility

You are responsible for holding the rights to any video you publish through the tool — your own recordings, or content you are licensed to use. The tool does not grant you rights to anyone else's work.
